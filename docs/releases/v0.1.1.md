## Mazzuola v0.1.1 — All shapes (alpha 1)

Every primitive shape and the rock, drawn the same way as the box in v0.1.0.

**Try this:**

- **Primitives** (Shift+B), or **Create → Primitives / Rock**: pick a shape in
  Properties, drag a rectangle in a 2D view, adjust it with the handles, Return creates
  it. The preview is the real shape, in all four views.
- **The shapes:** box (segments per side), plane (rows, columns, diagonals), cylinder
  and cone (sides, segments, cap rings), tube (sides, segments, hole size), torus (sides,
  tube sides, tube width), sphere (sides, stacks), geosphere (iterations). Each keeps its
  own settings.
- **Every shape fills the box you draw exactly**, whatever the number of sides. Round
  shapes stand along the view you drew them in: drawn in Top they stand upright (a
  torus lies flat), drawn in Front they face you. A torus's tube is as thick as the box
  is along that direction, so you see it while you adjust.
- **The rock:** faces (about how many triangles), roughness, flat bottom with its
  height, join into quads, seed and **New seed** (a different rock every time you
  press it; the same seed always gives the same rock).
- A new shape takes its depth from the last shape you made or selected (a flat plane
  doesn't count: then it's 64 cm).

Next: v0.1.2, points, edges and faces (the Vertex tool, dragging single points, the
arrows). Tell me what feels wrong first.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
