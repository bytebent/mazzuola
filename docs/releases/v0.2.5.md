## Mazzuola v0.2.5 — Clean up (alpha 2)

**Try this:**

- **Delete** now keeps the model closed:
  - **Point mode:** the points dissolve (the faces round each one join without it).
  - **Edge mode:** the edges dissolve and the faces on both sides join. Delete a loop
    cut's edges and the box is back as it was.
  - **Face mode:** selected faces next to each other join into one; a face on its own
    shrinks to a point (a box's top becomes a roof).
  - **Piece mode:** pieces are deleted, as before.
- **Mesh menu**, new:
  - **Merge at centre** / **Merge at first selected point** (the point you picked first).
  - **Collapse edges** (each to its middle).
  - **Weld by distance...** (a small window: selected points closer than 0.1 cm become
    one; it shows how many go).
  - **Dissolve** (the same as Delete above).
  - **Tear open**: the old Delete, which leaves a hole.
  - **Fill**: select 3 or 4 points round a hole for a new face, turned the same way as
    its neighbours.
  - **Detach**: the selected faces get their own points, so they can be moved away.
  - **Flip faces**, **Flip diagonal** (which way a four-cornered face is split).
  - **Triangulate** / **Join into quads** (flat triangles side by side).
  - **Double-sided on / off**.
- When one can't be done (it would open a closed model, the faces are too bent...), a
  message says why and nothing changes.

Next: v0.2.6 "Bevel, bridge, mirror", planned with you first.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
