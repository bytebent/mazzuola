## Mazzuola v0.0.1 — fixes from the first Sidecar test

- **Pen taps now click where the pen touches.** A tap from the Apple Pencil (through
  Sidecar) or a drawing tablet comes without a pointer move first, so clicks landed
  where the pointer had been before. Mazzuola now reads the tap's real position.
- **Panels move only by their title bar.** Touching an empty part of a panel that's in
  its own window (e.g. the texture view on the iPad) no longer drags the whole window.
- **No crash when a display goes away** (e.g. turning Sidecar off with a panel or the
  app on the iPad): panels in their own windows come back into the main window.
- **Help → Pen and mouse test:** a button to click with the mouse and the pen, and what
  Mazzuola receives (position, buttons, pen pressure and tilt). Useful to check the
  iPad setup.
## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
