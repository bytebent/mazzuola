## Mazzuola v0.2.7 — Mirror mode (alpha 2)

**New:**

- **Mirror mode** (**Shift+M**, or Mesh → Mirror mode). The mirror plane goes through
  the world origin: X to start, or pick **Mesh → Mirror plane X / Y / Z**. It shows as a
  dashed line in the 2D views and a see-through plane in the 3D view.
  - Picking something also picks its twin on the other side, so every command that works
    on the selection (Extrude, Inset, Bevel, Dissolve, Subdivide…) works on both sides.
  - Moving, turning and scaling are mirrored: drag on one side and the other follows the
    mirrored way. The same goes for the Vertex tool, the arrows, Move / rotate / scale
    (Cmd+T) and **Push/pull** (the twin face is pushed too).
  - Points on the plane stay on it.
  - Points without a twin move alone; the status bar says how many there are.
- **Mesh → Make symmetric…**: choose the plane and the side to keep; the other side is
  rebuilt as its mirror. Faces across the plane are cut along it. A live preview shows
  the result before you press Return.

**Good to know:**

- Which side "leads" is the side you pressed the mouse on.
- Extrude set to **Straight X** (along the mirror plane's axis) sends both sides the same
  way; **Along normals**, the default, is mirrored correctly.
- Draw, Cut and Primitives are not mirrored yet (planned for later).

This completes alpha 2 (milestone 2, modelling).

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
