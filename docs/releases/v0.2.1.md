## Mazzuola v0.2.1 — Edges and points (alpha 2)

A small update after your first try of v0.2.0.

**Try this:**

- **Push/pull on edges and points:** switch to Edge mode (or Vertex mode) with
  Push/pull on, and drag an edge (or a point). It moves along the surface's normal
  there (halfway between the faces that meet at it), and the faces around it tilt.
  No new faces. Good for roof ridges, wedges and slopes. Grid steps, a typed distance
  in Properties, **Return** applies and **Esc** cancels, as with faces. In a 2D view
  it works when that direction isn't pointing straight at you; of points stacked on
  top of each other, the nearest one is taken.
- **Inset doesn't start again by itself:** after Return, changing the width or depth
  no longer starts a second inset inside the one you just made. Click faces to inset
  again; the settings you typed are kept for them.

About the face count: one square inset makes 5 faces (the inner one and a frame of
4). The status bar counts triangles, two for every four-cornered face, so it shows
10 there.

Next: v0.2.2 "Draw on faces", planned with you click by click first.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
