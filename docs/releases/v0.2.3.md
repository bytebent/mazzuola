## Mazzuola v0.2.3 — Outlines and holes (alpha 2)

**Try this:**

- **Holes:** draw a rectangle or circle on a wall, then Push/pull it in. When it
  reaches the face on the other side it goes straight through and stops there: both
  shapes go and four walls join them (Properties says "Through to the other side: a
  hole"; it also tells you beforehand how far that is). A door drawn from the floor
  works too: the floor under it opens. Pushed less far, it's a recess as before.
- **Outline** (fourth choice in Draw, in a 2D view): click points (grid snapping as
  usual; near X / Y / Z it clicks onto the axis), then click the first point again to
  close it. Click more outlines inside it for holes. **Return** makes the flat shape,
  facing you; switch to Push/pull to make it solid, holes and all. **Backspace** takes
  a point back. **Double-sided** in Properties for things seen from both sides.
- **A wall is one surface now:** once a face has been cut, its pieces count as one
  face for drawing. A doorway next to a window, or a second window, just works.
  Rectangles line up with the world on walls, floors and roofs along an axis.
- Push/pull in a 2D view picks the drawn (selected) shape over the wall it lies on.

Next: v0.2.4 "Cut and divide" (knife, loop cut, subdivide...), planned with you first.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
