## Mazzuola v0.2.2 — Draw on faces (alpha 2)

A Draw tool (Shift+D, button under Inset) to draw shapes onto faces, then push or
pull them with Push/pull.

**Try this:**

- **Rectangle** (in Properties): click a corner on a face, then the opposite corner
  (or press, drag and let go). It lines up with the face's edges. Type its width and
  height in Properties, or drag a corner. **Return** cuts it in; **Esc** cancels.
- **Circle**: click the centre, then the radius; segments in Properties (8 to start).
- **Line**: click from one edge of a face, click on, end on another edge, **Return**.
  **Backspace** takes the last point back.
- The shape becomes its own face(s); the rest of the face is split into 3- and
  4-corner faces, and the faces next to it get the new points too, so the model stays
  closed. The new shape is selected: switch to Push/pull and pull it out or push it in
  (all of the shape goes, even if it is several faces).
- It must fit in the one face. Touching the face's edge is fine (a door from the
  floor). Too big, or a line that stops inside: it shows red, Properties says why, and
  Return does nothing.
- **Snapping**: the grid, and a small marker when it catches something else: green a
  point, cyan a midpoint, red an edge, blue the face's centre. While drawing a line, a
  piece close to X, Y or Z clicks onto it and turns that axis's colour; hold **Shift**
  to keep that direction.
- Works in the 3D view on any face, and in the 2D views on faces facing you.

The face splitting isn't always pretty yet (long thin triangles round a circle on a
big face). Tell me where it bothers you.

Next: v0.2.3 "Outlines and holes" (shapes drawn in a view, holes, push/pull through a
shape). We plan it with you first.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
