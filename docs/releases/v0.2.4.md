## Mazzuola v0.2.4 — Cut and divide (alpha 2)

**Try this:**

- **Cut tool** (Shift+X, button under Draw), two modes in Properties:
  - **Knife:** click points on edges or corners (a marker shows the snap: green a
    point, cyan a midpoint, red on an edge). Two clicks in a row must be on the same
    face, which is cut between them; keep clicking round corners onto other faces.
    **Return** cuts, **Backspace** takes a point back, **Esc** cancels.
  - **Loop cut:** point at an edge and the ring of cuts shows right round the model
    through the four-cornered faces in line with it (it stops at triangles). Click to
    pick it, type how many cuts (1) and where (50 %), **Return**. The new edges are
    selected.
- **Slide:** in the Vertex tool, **Alt**+drag a point. It moves along the one of its
  edges closest to the way you drag, in grid steps when snapping.
- **Mesh menu**, each with a small window (Return applies, Esc cancels):
  **Subdivide...** (faces into N x M, 2 x 2 to start), **Divide edges...** (into N),
  **Poke...** (a point in the middle of each face, raised by a typed height: a spike
  or a low pyramid), **Connect two points** (two selected points on one face: a new
  edge between them).
- Faces next to a cut always get the new points too, so the model stays closed.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
