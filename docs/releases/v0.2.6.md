## Mazzuola v0.2.6 — Bevel, bridge, shell (alpha 2)

**New:**

- **Bevel** (in the Extrude tool, **Shift+E**): select edges, or points, and the tool
  switches to Bevel by itself (faces and open edges still extrude; Properties has
  **Extrude / Bevel** at the top). Type the **width** or drag the arrow; **Segments**
  1–3 round the edge. Where three bevelled edges meet (a box corner) you get a small
  corner face. A bevelled point cuts the corner off. Too wide: the preview turns red and
  Return does nothing. The new faces end up selected.
- **Mesh → Bridge…**: select two faces (or two blocks of faces): they're removed and
  joined by a tube. **Segments** adds rings along it, **Twist** turns the far end. In
  edge mode, select an edge on each of two holes to join the holes. Different numbers
  of corners on each side work too (some tube faces become triangles).
- **Mesh → Shell…**: gives an open surface (a box without a top, a plane) a wall:
  **Thickness** (2 cm to start), **Inward / Outward / Centred**. A closed model is
  refused with a message.

**Good to know:**

- **Cmd+A selects the whole piece.** To bevel every edge, use edge mode and drag a
  rectangle round the model.
- After a bevel, faces with five or more corners (like a box's end next to a bevelled
  edge) are split in two, so a diagonal line shows in the 2D views. It's flat and
  doesn't show in the 3D view.
- After **Undo** the tool's preview comes back at once (as with Extrude); **Esc**
  drops it.

Next: v0.2.7 "Mirror mode", as agreed.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
