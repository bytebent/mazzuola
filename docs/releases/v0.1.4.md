## Mazzuola v0.1.4 — Out of the app (alpha 1)

Models can now leave Mazzuola, and you can model over reference pictures next to a
175 cm person. This completes alpha 1.

**Try this:**

- **File → Export:** glTF binary (.glb), glTF (.gltf with a .bin beside it) or
  Wavefront (.obj with a .mtl beside it). The whole model, Y-up in metres (what
  Scalpellino and most tools expect), with the world origin as its zero point. Every
  edge is hard for now (smoothing groups come in milestone 3), quads are split the way
  they are drawn, and everything gets one plain grey material.
- **Export again** (Cmd+Shift+E): the same file and format, no questions; the status
  bar says where it went. The first time, it asks where to save. Cmd+E stays free for
  Frame selection, as in Scalpellino.
- **Reference images:** **Image...** in a 2D view's header loads a PNG, JPG, TGA or BMP.
  It first fills the view; then type its real width in cm. While its window is open,
  drag the picture to move it or a corner to resize it (proportions kept, grid steps
  when snapping is on). Also: rotation, transparency, behind or in front of the model.
  Views facing the same way share it; the 3D view has none; it is never picked while
  you model and is not saved. **Image** in the header opens its window again.
- **View → Scale figure:** a see-through 175 cm person (the same one as Scalpellino's)
  beside the origin on the −X side, just outside the model. Never exported or saved.

Next: alpha 1 is done. Milestone 2 (modelling: draw on faces, push/pull, extrude...)
gets planned with you first, click by click. Tell me what feels wrong first.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
