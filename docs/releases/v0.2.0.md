## Mazzuola v0.2.0 — Push and pull (alpha 2)

The first modelling tools: grow and carve shapes out of their faces. Each has a button
on the left, under Primitives, and is in the Tools menu.

**Try this:**

- **Push/pull** (Shift+F): drag a face along its normal. In the 3D view any face; in a
  2D view drag a face you see edge-on (a line), like a brush side. The distance goes
  in grid steps and shows in Properties, where you can type it. Releasing the mouse
  keeps the preview; **Return** applies, **Esc** cancels; pressing another face applies
  the pending push first.
  - A box's side stretches the box (no new faces). Faces whose neighbours aren't at
    right angles get new side faces; hold **Cmd** when you start dragging to always get
    new faces. A flat shape (one face on its own) becomes a solid.
  - Too far (faces would turn over): it shows red and Return does nothing.
- **Extrude** (Shift+E): select faces, and a preview shows at once (16 cm to start).
  Drag its arrow or type the distance; Region / Each face, Along normals / Straight
  X Y Z, Keep original (a flat shape becomes a solid). **Return** applies and selects
  the new end faces, ready to extrude again. In edge mode, open edges (a face on one
  side only) extrude straight into a strip of new faces.
- **Inset** (Shift+I): a smaller copy of each face inside it, with a ring of faces
  around. Type the width and, optionally, a depth: the inner face goes in with straight
  walls, leaving a flat frame (negative: it comes out). Region / Each face. Too wide:
  red. **Return** applies and selects the inner faces.
- While Extrude or Inset is on, clicks and rectangles still select (nothing moves);
  the preview follows the new selection.

Next: v0.2.1 "Draw on faces" (lines, rectangles and circles on faces, outlines with
holes, snapping to points and edges, push/pull through a shape to make a hole). We
plan it click by click after you've tried this.

## Download

- **Intel Mac:** `Mazzuola-intel.zip`
- **Apple Silicon Mac (M1/M2/...):** not built automatically; run the `apple-silicon`
  workflow in the Actions tab to get one.

## First launch

Unzip to get `Mazzuola.app`. It isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate, so
macOS blocks it the first time:

1. Double-click it once (macOS refuses to open it).
2. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**.

Or in Terminal: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /path/to/Mazzuola.app`

Controls are listed in the README.
